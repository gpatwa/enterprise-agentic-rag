# ADS-072: Context/Ontology Retrieval and Token-Efficiency Evaluation

Status: **Review** (evaluated locally against an in-memory stand-in for OpenSearch; thresholds **proposed, not approved**)
Milestone: M7 (wave 7B)
Depends on: ADS-019, ADS-070

## Decisions made under the user's delegation ("make the decision")

1. **Run the real adapter against a fake backend.** The corpus is indexed through `OpenSearchContextIndex` into `FakeSearchClient` (term overlap, `term`/`terms`/`must_not` filters).
   It is not BM25, so this shows the adapter, scope filters, and pack builder work, not that real relevance ranking does. No live OpenSearch was touched.
2. **A catalogue with traps.** 12 certified datasets, 2 uncertified lookalikes (near-identical descriptions), 1 stale asset, a second tenant with same-named datasets, 15 ontology
   nodes (metrics and dimensions with aliases), and 40 hand-written queries (20 dataset, 12 metric, 8 dimension). The corpus is pinned by digest and generated by the module.
3. **Six gates.** Top-3 recall >= 98% and token reduction >= 20% come from plan section 10; zero uncertified/foreign results, 100% provenance, 100% stale detection, and zero pack
   overruns are the implementing agent's proposals. All are **proposed**.
4. **Token baseline = sending the whole certified catalogue** with the same estimator as `build_context_pack`. The plan's baseline is the ADS-007 v1 number; that comparison is not made here.
5. **A finding about defence in depth.** With a backend that ignores every filter, the adapter's per-hit re-check still keeps leaks at zero (tested). The leak gate is shown to fail only
   when that re-check is removed (tested with a patched adapter).
